## Overview

This project develops a computational engineering platform for vehicle dynamics and suspension design. The goal is to make model assumptions, geometry, numerical methods and engineering evidence explicit rather than relying on opaque calculators.

## Technical direction

- Suspension and steering kinematics.
- Equilibrium and steady-cornering analyses.
- Parameter sensitivity, candidate comparison and operating-envelope studies.
- Reproducible configuration, validation and evidence reporting.

## Verification

Numerical convergence is not the same as physical correctness. The platform separates analytical verification from physical correlation and distinguishes provisional inputs from validated parameters.

An existing analytical comparison workflow provides a basis for future case studies. Physical correlation is outstanding; this is not a claim of predictive accuracy.
