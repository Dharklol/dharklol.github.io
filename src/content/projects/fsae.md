## Overview

Formula SAE provides a practical environment for designing, manufacturing and testing a small race car. My work has focused on steering and suspension engineering, particularly the interaction between geometry, steering effort, packaging and driver input.

## Technical direction

- Steering geometry and Ackermann trade-offs across the operating range.
- Rack placement, tie-rod geometry and steering ratio optimization under packaging constraints.
- Anti-roll bar characterization and the interpretation of experimental stiffness measurements.
- Mechanical design, drawing review and manufacturability.

## Engineering approach

I approach design changes as a balance of competing objectives: driver effort, kinematic behavior, packaging, stiffness, manufacturability and testability. Calculations and simulations guide the trade study; physical measurements are needed to establish installed performance.

This case study is an introductory summary. Detailed figures and test evidence will be added as they are reviewed.
