## Overview

Wydown is the umbrella for my independent quantitative research and related engineering work. It brings together research methodology, statistical modeling and the data systems required to investigate financial markets.

The work spans multiple research threads; the portfolio framework and the associated experiments have distinct development stages.

## Research

- Systematic portfolio strategies and reproducible experiment specifications.
- Market microstructure and conditional market response research.
- Credit-market transmission and structured-finance monitoring.

## Data and computation

- Real-time market data acquisition and event-driven recording.
- Order book reconstruction, integrity checking and latency benchmarking.
- Research infrastructure, repeatable experiments and constrained remote operations.
- Portfolio accounting, historical data provenance and risk controls.

## Research discipline

The aim is to evaluate hypotheses against explicitly defined data, assumptions and tests. Research results, paper trading and live operations are kept distinct. No investment performance or live trading capability is claimed here.
